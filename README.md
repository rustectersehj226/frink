## 🎯 What is frink?

frink is a powerful desktop application that helps you automate Claude Code and Codex, two popular AI coding assistants. Think of frink as your personal automation manager - it runs your AI agents automatically when specific events happen, like when you receive a new ticket, encounter an error, get a webhook notification, or at scheduled times.

This open-source tool runs directly on your computer (macOS and Linux), giving you full control over your AI workflows without needing to use the cloud or share your data.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚀 Run Agents on Tickets
When a new ticket comes in, frink can automatically launch your AI coding agent to analyze it, propose solutions, or even implement fixes. No more manually copying ticket details into your AI tools.

### 🔥 Error Handling Automation
frink monitors for errors and triggers your AI agents to investigate and resolve them instantly. This means less downtime and faster problem resolution.

### 📡 Webhook Integration
Connect frink to your existing webhooks. When events happen in your other tools, frink automatically kicks off your AI workflows without any manual intervention.

### ⏰ Scheduled Automations
Set up frink to run your AI agents at specific times. Whether it's daily code reviews, weekly reports, or hourly monitoring, frink handles it all on schedule.

### 🔄 Parallel Agent Execution
Run multiple AI agents simultaneously to tackle different tasks at once. This dramatically speeds up your workflow and increases productivity.

### 💻 Runs Locally
frink operates entirely on your machine. Your code, conversations, and data stay with you - no cloud uploads or third-party servers involved.
